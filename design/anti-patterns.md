# Yohaku Reminder Anti-Patterns

Yohaku Reminder must not become a colorful, busy, productivity-heavy reminder app.

When in doubt, remove anything that does not need to exist.

---

## Do Not Add

Do not add:

- colorful priorities
- badges
- productivity scores
- statistics
- streaks
- habit tracking
- kanban boards
- project dashboards
- AI suggestions on the main screen
- decorative illustrations
- character elements
- loud empty states

Do not make the app convenient at the cost of visual silence.

---

## Visual Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- strong colors
- gradients
- decorative illustrations
- colorful priority labels
- heavy borders
- bold cards
- dense layouts
- dashboard-like UI
- playful animations
- excessive explanations

---

## Screen Anti-Patterns

The main screen should not show:

- motivational text
- weather
- task counters
- category summaries
- analytics
- progress
- unnecessary headings

Do not use large headings like:

```text
Tasks
Today
My Reminders
```

---

## Reminder Anti-Patterns

Do not use:

- thick borders
- colored cards
- category colors
- icons for every reminder
- bright completion colors
- urgent overdue styling
- complex recurrence controls

---

## Form Anti-Patterns

Avoid complex forms.

Do not show too many controls at once.

Avoid large primary buttons.

Do not show unused fields.

If a field is empty, hide it instead of showing placeholder noise.

---

## Copy Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- long explanations
- motivational copy
- casual jokes
- overly friendly text
- productivity buzzwords
- urgent language

Do not over-explain the UI.

---

## Product Direction Anti-Patterns

Yohaku Reminder should not feel like:

- a productivity dashboard
- a colorful task manager
- a habit tracker
- a project management app
- a business tool

The goal is not to manage everything.

The goal is to remember only what matters.
