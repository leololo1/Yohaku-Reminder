# Yohaku Reminder Component Rules

## Floating Add Button

Use a small white circular button with a soft shadow.

Size:

```text
48-56pt
```

Style:

- white fill
- subtle shadow
- thin plus icon
- no strong accent color

The plus button should be small and quiet.

---

## Icon Buttons

Use thin line icons only.

Icon color:

```text
#777777
```

Icon stroke should be thin.

Avoid filled icons.

---

## Reminder Rows

Each reminder row may include:

- completion mark
- reminder title
- date or time
- notification timing if set
- memo preview only when it exists

Rows should be separated by very subtle dividers.

Do not use colored cards.
Do not use priority colors.
Do not use icons for every reminder.

The list should feel like text placed on paper.

---

## Completion Mark

The completion mark should be a quiet circle.

Open state:

```text
thin hairline circle
```

Completed state:

```text
soft gray fill
thin check mark
```

Do not use bright green or celebratory animation.

---

## Date and Time Fields

Date and time selection should feel like choosing from paper, not configuring a machine.

Use simple rows:

```text
日付
時刻
通知
メモ
```

Keep values quiet and tabular where helpful.

---

## Notification Choices

Notification choices must be sparse.

Allowed choices:

- なし
- 時刻
- 5分前
- 10分前
- 30分前
- 1時間前
- 前日

Do not show complex repeat rules unless the product explicitly needs them.

---

## Forms

Reminder forms should feel like writing on blank paper.

Avoid complex forms.

Do not show too many controls at once.

Place save and close actions quietly at the top.

Avoid large primary buttons.

---

## Detail Rows

Use icons only if they are very thin and minimal.

Text-only layout is preferred.

If a field is empty, hide it instead of showing placeholder noise.
